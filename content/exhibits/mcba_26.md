Established in 2009, the MCBA Prize is an international artist’s book award that honors excellence in new work from across the sweeping spectrum of book art. This year, they received 146 submissions from 18 countries, from the Bahamas to Kazakhstan. Honored to be included in the show as a semifinalist for the collaborative work [Arise by Any Other Name](https://servanebriand.com/gallery/arise-serial/), with Patrick Fenton and Samuel Mignot.

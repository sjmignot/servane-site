The San Francisco Opera and the HandBookbinders of California organized an exhibit during Pride month to display artists books and fine bindings made from pieces of a retired Rainbow Flag donated by the Castro Merchants Association of San Francisco. [Read more about the project](https://handbookbinders.org/prideflagproject) on the HBC website.
